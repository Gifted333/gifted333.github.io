# Privacy Policy for Go and Verify: The Truth Seeker

**Last updated:** September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Gifted Intelligent Solutions ("we," "our," or "us") handles information when you play *Go and Verify: The Truth Seeker* (the "Game"), available on Google Play.

By downloading, installing, or playing the Game, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Game does not require you to create an account. We do not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifiers, unless you voluntarily contact us (for example, by email for support).

## 2. Information Collected Automatically (via AdMob)

The Game displays advertisements using **Google AdMob**. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob may automatically collect:

- Advertising ID (AAID) or other device identifiers
- IP address (used to determine approximate location, such as country or city)
- Device information (model, operating system version)
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
- Diagnostic and crash data related to ad serving

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly. Google may use it to:

- Deliver and personalize ads (where permitted)
- Measure ad performance
- Prevent fraud and abuse
- Limit how often you see the same ad

For details on how Google handles this data, see Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [AdMob Privacy & Messaging](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).

## 3. How We Use Information

We do not use any personal data beyond what is needed to display ads through AdMob and to respond to support requests you send us directly.

## 4. Sharing of Information

- **Google AdMob** — Ad-related data described above is shared with Google as our ad service provider.
- **Legal requirements** — We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users' safety.
- We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 5. Your Choices

- You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization on Android).
- You can reset your Advertising ID at any time through your device settings.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us so we can address it.

## 7. Data Retention

We do not retain any personal data ourselves. AdMob-related data is retained according to Google's own data retention policies.
